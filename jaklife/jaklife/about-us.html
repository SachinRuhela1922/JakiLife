JakLife Science's core vision revolves around nurturing a prosperous nation through a strong commitment to the well-being of animals. At the heart of JakLife Science's principles lies a deep-seated belief in the importance of sharing knowledge and raising awareness, particularly in improving animal health.
To translate this vision into reality, JakLife Science consistently orchestrates a diverse array of awareness programs and campaigns. The company aims to disseminate invaluable insights and information concerning animal health to its clientele through these initiatives. By arming individuals with the wisdom garnered from these programs, JakLife Science empowers them to effectively identify and address various animal health issues. Consequently, this encourages them to take proactive measures in addressing these challenges head-on.

From the Founder's Desk

Suman Gupta Managing Director

In 2006, I embarked on a journey driven by a vision: to establish an organization rooted in integrity and collaboration, dedicated to delivering innovative, high-quality and affordable animal health products. Despite initial warnings about the challenges of entrepreneurship, I persisted. Over the past 28 years, our path has been marked by both significant milestones and remarkable challenges. Throughout, my focus has remained unwavering: assembling a team of imaginative minds. I firmly believe that creativity is the catalyst for innovation, and the collective efforts of our Rodec family have been instrumental in our success.

Over the years mission remains consistent: to deliver groundbreaking, cost-effective healthcare solutions for animals across India. The journey for JakLife Science is far from over; it's just beginning! Looking forward, I am filled with pride in our achievements so far. Yet, I am humbled by the challenges that lie ahead. The animal healthcare industry is ever-evolving and we must remain adaptable and innovative to stay ahead. "If you can't hear, feel the agony of mother animals." Let's be their voice, their hope, and their champion for a healthier future. Onwards and upwards!
